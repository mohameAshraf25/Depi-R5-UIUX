##  Project Overview
[A mobile application designed to help Egyptian citizens understand and prepare the required documents for government services before visiting a government office.
The app provides a clear, simple, and organized checklist of the required documents, fees, procedures, and important information for each government service, helping users avoid missing documents, unnecessary trips, and long waiting times.]

---

## Project Objectives
- [Reduce unnecessary visits]  
- [Clarify document requirements]  
- [Simplify government procedures]  
- [Save users' time and effort]
- [Reduce confusion and uncertainty] 

---

##  Project Scope
- [Government service discovery and search]  
- [Service categories and browsing]  
- [Required documents and personalized checklists]  
- [Step-by-step procedures]  
- [Service locations and relevant authorities]
